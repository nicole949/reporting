Per period

Hours per period from the HubSpot Services object · allowances = monthly hour allowances (included hours per month, from start until expiration or cancellation) · packages = one-time hour packages spread evenly from start to expiration · used = hours logged against the service (Jira sync) · potential overusage = what one-time packages and stackable allowances may additionally draw in a period (factor × the period's allocation, limited by the monthly cap) · open deals = professional-service deals not yet won · capacity = team hours available for professional services.
