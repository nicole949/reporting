HubSpot sequences: attribution via HubSpot's sequence ID · steps inferred from distinct emails ordered by first send (📂 = AI-personalized subjects grouped into categories) · 📅💼🏆 = companies with a discovery / deal / customer after the sequence first reached them · replies exclude auto-replies · CTA & collateral determined by AI from the email bodies. Instantly campaigns are discovered automatically from the Instantly API (nightly): enrolled = leads, open / reply = unique leads per emails sent, dates = campaign creation; they carry no HubSpot persona / ICP / seniority data, so they are hidden while a segment filter is active. Persona / ICP / seniority filters switch every sequence's numbers to the contacts of that segment (one segment at a time).
